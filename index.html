Herb & Organics — Herbal Care Guide

Herb & Organics is a family-run herbal care brand from Kot Addu, Pakistan. Its handmade range includes Nabat Herbal Soap, Nabat Shampoo and Nabat Hair Oil, made with traditional herbal ingredients for gentle everyday skin and hair care.

Start with the soap

Nabat Herbal Soap is a gentle handmade wash for sensitive skin. Use it daily as a face and body wash, and follow with the herbal shampoo on wash days.

Weekly hair-oil ritual

Warm a little Nabat Hair Oil, massage it into the scalp, leave it for an hour, then wash with Herb & Organics Nabat Shampoo. Once a week is enough for most hair types.
